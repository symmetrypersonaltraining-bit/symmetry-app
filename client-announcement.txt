🚀 BIG UPGRADE — the Symmetry food logger just got a total rebuild.

It's faster, simpler, and honestly kind of fun. Here's everything it can do and how to log no matter what your day looks like.


THE BASICS — ONE TAP

Your day is a simple checklist of your meals.

Ate a meal exactly as planned? Just tap the circle next to it.

That's it — it's logged and your macros update instantly.


ONLY ATE PART OF IT?

Tap the meal (or the three dots on it) and choose ¾, ½, ¼, or Skipped.

Your numbers adjust automatically.


ATE SOMETHING OFF-PLAN? THREE EASY WAYS:

1) Snap a photo — tap the camera, take a picture of your plate, and the AI reads the macros for you.

2) Type it — just write what you ate ("8 oz chicken, 1 cup rice, 1 tbsp olive oil") and it figures out the macros.

3) Say it — tap the mic and just talk.

Eating out? Take a photo of the receipt or the item and it pulls the real restaurant numbers.


SEARCH ANY FOOD OR SCAN A BARCODE

Tap search and pull from a database of 100,000+ foods (and growing).

Or scan the barcode right off the package and log it in one tap.


MAKE IT YOURS

Swap a planned meal for something custom.

Build your own meals once and save them to reuse anytime.

Add an extra meal or snack anywhere in your day.

Copy a meal into another slot.

Reorder your meals — press and hold, then drag.

Made a mistake? Un-log any meal, fix it, and log it again.


SEE YOUR PROGRESS

The card at the top shows your calories and protein / carbs / fat for the day.

Tap Today, 1W, 4W, or 8W to see your averages over time.

You'll also see your Adherence and your Logging Rate — how consistent you're being.


GROCERY LIST + MEAL PREP

Open the menu (the three dots) and tap Grocery & Prep.

Generate a Grocery PDF and a full Meal Prep PDF — the prep sheet shows exactly what goes in each container.

Send it to yourself or whoever shops and cooks.


YOUR AI COACH

Tap the ✦ button anytime.

Ask it anything — "How's my protein today?" or "Swap my dinner for something with the same macros."

It can log, swap, move, and answer questions for you. It's like having me in your pocket 24/7.


ONE ASK

Log every meal today — even the off-plan ones. Especially the off-plan ones.

The more you log, the more I can see, and the faster we get you to your goals.

Let's go. 💪
