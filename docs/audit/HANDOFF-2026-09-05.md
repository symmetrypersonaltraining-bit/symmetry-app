## ✅ FIXED THE SAME NIGHT — the calendar-sync guard

Dustin asked whether a client moving a supervised workout breaks the job that
pulls those workouts onto his calendar's dates. It did, and it had since long
before that night. He said go, and it is done — full write-up in
**`docs/CALENDAR-WINS-WHEN-NEWER-2026-09-05.md`**.

Short version: the guard asked *"has a person ever moved this?"* when it should
have asked *"who moved it most recently?"*. `scheduled_workouts` now carries
`moved_at`, stamped by the trigger on every date change, and the sync's guard
gained `or a.updated_at > sw.moved_at`. A client's move sticks until he moves
the appointment; then the calendar takes it back.

Backed up to `bak_scheduled_workouts_20260905` (5,716 rows) first. Dry run after
the change moved nothing; a simulated appointment change correctly pulled a
previously-stranded session back, and the simulation was reverted exactly.

**One thing left for his eye:** the 15 rows that were stranded sit exactly one
day after their appointments — Lauren Standefer (6, linked) and Sariah Duncan
(9, orphans). None will move on their own. If that one-day offset is not
deliberate they want realigning by hand.

## Standing rules that bit tonight — worth knowing before they bite again

- **The ship bridge.** Cloud sessions cannot push. Commit → `git bundle create`
  into `/tmp` then copy to outputs → `device_commit_files` the bundle THEN
  `SHIP-NOW` → poll `SHIP-RESULT.txt`. Two things caught me: a **full** bundle
  is 24 MB and the bridge caps files at 20 MB, so always bundle
  `origin/main..main main`; and another session pushed mid-flight, so on
  "not a fast-forward" rebase onto `origin/main` and resend rather than forcing.
- **Trainer identity test.** Do not put his name inside a prompt string —
  `COACH_FIRST_NAME` or a code comment. It failed the suite twice.
- **Tests re-anchor, they do not weaken.** `moveWorkoutAnywhere` asserted a
  literal sentence I deliberately narrowed. The premise was still right, so the
  fix was to re-anchor it AND assert the new exception, not to delete the check.
