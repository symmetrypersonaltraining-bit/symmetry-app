### The outage, in order of what actually happened

**The database ran out of CPU, and the imports drank it.**

Your Supabase instance is burstable — it earns CPU credits while idle and spends
them under load. `off-bulk-import` and `off-micros-backfill` pushed 1.5M rows
through an 853 MB table every 60 seconds for hours. Credits gone, instance
throttled to a fraction of one processor, and then everything on it degraded at
once:

- Auth `GET /user` — **10 to 65 seconds**, 504 on about half of all calls
- PostgREST — timing out on roughly half my queries all night
- Realtime — a 13.6-second internal query
- Your pages — the 504 you saw

The proof, on an idle database with nothing else running:

```
select count(*) from generate_series(1, 3000000)
  06:47Z  4.26s      ← healthy is 0.4–0.7s
  06:48Z  6.58s
  07:50Z  2.77s      ← imports off, credits refilling
  08:16Z  1.15s
  08:35Z  3.52s      ← choppy, not a clean line
```

And a real query — 46 rows, correct index, 31 pages read — took **4,631 ms**,
of which **844 ms was planning**. Thirty-one pages cannot take 4.6 seconds.
There was nothing wrong with the queries. There was nothing left to run them
with.

**I got this wrong twice before I got it right**, and you should know in what
order. First I said the imports were saturating the connection pool — right

### 🔴 The one I broke myself

The 4-second cap I added at 04:56 had a flaw. Racing a call against a timer does
not cancel it — and when that call is *refreshing* your session, Supabase spends
the old refresh token the instant it issues the new one, and the new one lands
on a response nobody is holding. Old token spent, new one lost, session dead.

That is the same fault I fixed for you in August with `redirectKeepingSession`,
reintroduced from underneath. And while auth was taking 10–65s against a 4s cap,
**every refresh in those hours was abandoned mid-flight.** Your session died of
it at 06:35Z.

Fixed in `331aed1`: cut a call short only when there is nothing to lose. No
session cookie means nothing to refresh, so 4s still applies. A request carrying
a session waits 15s — longer than Supabase's own 10s deadline, so we never
abandon a rotation in flight.
