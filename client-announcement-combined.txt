🚀 BIG SYMMETRY APP UPDATE IS LIVE

Your app got a major upgrade on both the food logger AND the workout logger. Here's everything that's new and how to use it.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
🥗 FOOD LOGGER — REBUILT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

LOG IN ONE TAP — Your day is a simple checklist and every meal now shows exactly what's in it. Ate a meal as planned? Tap the circle. Done — your macros update instantly.

ONLY HAD PART? Tap the meal and pick 3/4, 1/2, 1/4, or Skipped. It adjusts automatically.

ATE OFF-PLAN? Three easy ways:
- Snap a photo — AI reads the macros off your plate
- Type it — "8 oz chicken, 1 cup rice" and it does the math
- Say it — tap the mic and just talk
Eating out? Photo the receipt and it pulls the real restaurant numbers.

SEARCH OR SCAN — Search a database of 100,000+ foods, or scan the barcode right off the package.

MAKE IT YOURS — Swap a meal for something custom, build and save your own meals to reuse, add a snack anywhere, press-and-drag to reorder, and un-log anything to fix it.

BUILD YOUR OWN PLAN WITH AI — Open the ⋯ menu and tap "Build my own plan with AI." Answer a couple questions and it builds a full plan to your goals, then switches you to it instantly. (I can still see it and jump in anytime.)

TRACK YOUR PROGRESS — The top card shows today's calories and macros. Tap Today / 1W / 4W / 8W for your averages, adherence, and logging streak.

GROCERY + MEAL PREP — ⋯ menu → Grocery & Prep builds a grocery list PDF and a full meal-prep sheet (exactly what goes in each container) you can send to whoever shops or cooks.

YOUR AI COACH — Tap the ✦ button anytime to ask questions, get feedback, or have it log and adjust things for you.

🏋️ WORKOUT LOGGER — CLEANED UP
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

- All your sets stay on screen while you log — the keyboard won't cover your reps and the page won't jump around.
- Your last weight auto-fills for each movement, so you always know the number to beat.
- Tap the chart icon (📊) on any exercise to scroll your full history — every date, weight, and rep.
- Tap the flag (🚩) at the top of a workout to send me feedback or a question anytime.

NEW — CREATE / REPLACE WORKOUT WITH AI — Open any workout and tap "Create / Replace Workout" at the top:
- Replace today's workout — traveling or missed it? AI builds a substitute that fits your program.
- Use what I have — snap a photo or describe your equipment and AI designs around it.
- Log something I did — volleyball, yoga, a hike — log it and it counts.
You can speak or type on any of it, and anything you create is saved to your own library to reuse. Find it at the top of any workout.
